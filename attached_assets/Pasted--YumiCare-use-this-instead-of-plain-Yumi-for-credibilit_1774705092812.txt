
YumiCare (use this instead of plain “Yumi” for credibility)
🎯 Problem Statement
Pregnant patients often have fragmented medical records across clinics, cities, and formats.
In emergency situations, doctors lack:
immediate access to patient history
allergy and medication data
pregnancy progression insights
This results in:
delayed treatment
increased maternal/fetal risk
poor continuity of care
💡 Solution
YumiCare is a secure, AI-assisted maternal health platform that:
stores complete pregnancy medical records
enables QR-based emergency access
provides AI analysis of ultrasound & reports
builds a continuous medical timeline
